ACME CORP — CUSTOMER REFUND POLICY
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Document Version: 3.2

SECTION 1: ELIGIBILITY FOR REFUNDS

Customers are eligible for a full refund within 30 days of purchase, provided the product
is returned in its original, unused condition with all packaging intact. Digital products
and downloadable software are eligible for a refund within 14 days of purchase, but only
if the license key has not been activated. Subscription services (monthly or annual plans)
are eligible for a prorated refund if cancellation occurs within the first 7 days of a
billing cycle.

Custom-made or personalized items are non-refundable unless they arrive damaged or
defective. Gift cards and store credit are non-refundable and cannot be exchanged for cash.

SECTION 2: REFUND PROCESS

To request a refund, customers must submit a refund request through the online support
portal or contact customer service at support@acmecorp.example. The request must include
the original order number, the reason for the return, and photographic evidence if the
item is being returned due to damage or defect.

Once a refund request is approved, refunds are processed within 5-7 business days. The
refunded amount is credited back to the original payment method used at checkout. If the
original payment method is no longer valid (e.g., an expired credit card), the refund will
be issued as store credit instead.

Shipping costs are non-refundable except in cases where the return is due to a company
error, such as shipping the wrong item or a manufacturing defect.

SECTION 3: EXCHANGES

Customers may request an exchange instead of a refund within the same 30-day window.
Exchanges are subject to product availability. If the requested replacement item is of
higher value, the customer will be charged the price difference. If the replacement item
is of lower value, the difference will be issued as store credit.

SECTION 4: DEFECTIVE OR DAMAGED ITEMS

If an item arrives damaged or defective, the customer must report this within 48 hours of
delivery. Acme Corp will cover all return shipping costs for defective items and will
issue either a full refund or a free replacement, at the customer's choice. Defective
items do not need to be in original packaging to qualify for this policy.

SECTION 5: LATE OR MISSING REFUNDS

If a refund has not appeared within 10 business days of approval, customers should first
check with their bank or credit card provider, as processing times vary by institution.
If the refund still has not posted after this period, customers should contact support
with their order number for further investigation.

SECTION 6: INTERNATIONAL ORDERS

International customers are responsible for return shipping costs unless the return is
due to a company error. Customs duties and import taxes paid at the time of purchase are
non-refundable, as these are collected by the destination country's government and not by
Acme Corp.

SECTION 7: POLICY EXCEPTIONS

Acme Corp reserves the right to make exceptions to this policy on a case-by-case basis,
particularly in situations involving extenuating circumstances. Any exception must be
approved in writing by a customer service manager and documented in the customer's
account notes.
