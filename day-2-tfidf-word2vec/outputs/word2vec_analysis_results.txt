Note: Word2Vec similarity may still be low/moderate here because our training
corpus is very small (~25k tokens) -- Word2Vec typically needs millions of tokens
to learn rich semantic relationships. Word embeddings from models pretrained on
billions of words (e.g., Google News 300d vectors) show MUCH stronger synonym
similarity. This is an honest limitation of training on a small corpus, and is
itself a useful lesson: embedding QUALITY scales with training DATA VOLUME.
