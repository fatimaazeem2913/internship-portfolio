--- WHY THE FIXED WINDOW CAN'T CAPTURE LONG-RANGE CONTEXT ---

A trigram model's prediction depends ONLY on the last 2 words -- it has zero
memory of anything earlier in the sentence. Concretely:
  Sentence: "the virus that scientists in Wuhan first identified in late 2019 spread"
  When predicting the word after "spread", a trigram model only looks at
  ("2019", "spread") -- it has completely forgotten "virus", "scientists", and "Wuhan"
  even though those words are critical for understanding what comes next.
  Increasing n (4-gram, 5-gram...) helps marginally but the context window is
  still FIXED and finite, and the data sparsity problem gets rapidly worse --
  most higher-order n-grams are never seen in training data at all, forcing
  the model to fall back on smoothing alone. This fixed-window ceiling is
  exactly what motivates sequence models (RNN/LSTM) that carry a running
  hidden state across the WHOLE sequence instead of a fixed lookback window.
