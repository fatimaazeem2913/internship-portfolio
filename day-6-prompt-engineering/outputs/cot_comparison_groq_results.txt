Step 1: Define the variables.
Let's denote the cost of the ball as B (in dollars) and the cost of the bat as T (in dollars).

Step 2: Translate the given information into equations.
We have two pieces of information:
1. The total cost of the bat and the ball is $1.10.
2. The bat costs $1.00 more than the ball.

This can be translated into the following equations:
1. T + B = 1.10 (The total cost of the bat and the ball is $1.10)
2. T = B + 1.00 (The bat costs $1.00 more than the ball)

Step 3: Solve the equations.
We can substitute the second equation into the first equation to find the value of B (the cost of the ball).
Substituting T = B + 1.00 into the equation T + B = 1.10 gives us:
(B + 1.00) + B = 1.10

Step 4: Simplify and solve for B.
Combine like terms:
2B + 1.00 = 1.10

Subtract 1.00 from both sides to isolate the terms with B:
2B = 1.10 - 1.00
2B = 0.10

Divide both sides by 2 to solve for B:
B = 0.10 / 2
B = 0.05

Therefore, the ball costs $0.05.
